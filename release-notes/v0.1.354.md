## Fix: mock-stamped finds the stamps `anchors stamp` wrote

`mock-stamped` now ties a stamp to a double through the same resolution `anchors stamp`
uses: the project's aliases, its extensions, then the tail of the path.

It used to compare path suffixes. A double could stay "without stamp" right after
`anchors stamp` wrote its stamp, in two cases:
- an alias that maps into another folder, such as `@mif/shared/x` → `packages/shared/src/x.ts`;
- a directory resolved to its `index.ts`.

Everything in the v0.1.351 notes still applies: run `anchors migrate` once, crossing format 8.
