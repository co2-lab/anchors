# v0.2.9

- **`anchors verify --timing`.** A project's pre-commit took ten minutes over 105 staged files, and the hook's `verify` had no way to say which gate took them: `--timing` lived on `check` only. `verify` passes it on, so a slow pre-commit is measured as the hook runs it — `anchors verify --phase pre-commit --staged --timing` names how long each gate took and its slowest targets. (VPFVR)
