# v0.2.8

- **Saving the map takes a fraction of the memory.** yaml.v3's emitter keeps every event of a document until it ends, so a map marshalled whole held gigabytes: a project's 30 MB map allocated 3.5 GB to be saved, and `anchors test` held 3.2 GB of RSS after its runner ended. The map is now marshalled a node and an edge at a time, each in its place under its list's key, and its bytes are the same, so no map changes. The file on disk is read once and compared as bytes. On that 30 MB map, the save's heap went from 1.4 GB to 150 MB, and a load-and-save's peak RSS from 1.7 GB to 559 MB. Every command that writes the map benefits: `map build`, `check`, `anchors test`'s ingestion. (GRPRG)
