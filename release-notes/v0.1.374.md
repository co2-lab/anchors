## Fix: evidence kept by declaration survives the next change that proves nothing

`anchors keep-evidence` moved a file's evidence to its current content and moved the node's
revision with it, but left the node's evidence revisions — the content without what proves
nothing, such as the header's date — those of the previous content. The next rebuild compared
the new content's evidence revisions with those, found them changed, and dropped what the
declaration had kept. At MIF, `keep-evidence --lines` on 781 files whose imports alone had
changed, followed by `anchors touch` bumping their headers' dates, left 271 of them with no
mutation.

The node now takes the evidence revisions of the content kept. The same holds for the files
`check --fix` repairs and for the specs `anchors migrate` rewrites.

`keep-evidence` also brings back the mutation the map at HEAD holds for a file whose signal
holds none — a coverage run at the new content had started a new signal, and the command
found nothing to carry. A mutation measured again at the new content stays its own. To recover
files that lost their mutation this way, run `keep-evidence --lines` on them again before the
map is committed: the map at HEAD is where the mutation comes back from.

Everything in the v0.1.351 notes still applies: run `anchors migrate` once, crossing format 8.
