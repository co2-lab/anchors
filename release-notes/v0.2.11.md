# v0.2.11

- **A partial run's coverage speaks for its targets only.** A partial run's lcov — jest's `--findRelatedTests` with `--coverage` — reports every collected file: those the related tests never loaded at 0% (with `collectCoverageFrom`), and those they loaded measured by a few of their tests only. Taken whole, it replaced whole measurements with parts of one, a false `line-coverage` or `coverage-delta` waiting to happen. `anchors test --changed` now hands the impact path to the ingestion, and only those files take the coverage; the tests related to a file are every test that can reach it, so its measurement is whole. The other files are left as they were, and the run says how many. A partial ingestion by hand, with no targets, still takes the whole report. So a suite's `run_changed:` may add `--coverage` as it is. (NGSTI)
